Ask your AI assistant. Get your local paper's answer.

Connect Claude, ChatGPT or another AI assistant to Gazette's independent local journalism. When you ask about your suburb, your council or what's on nearby, it can answer from our reporting and link you straight to the story.

https://mcp.gazettenews.au/mcp

This is the only official address for Gazette's server. It's free, read-only and needs no sign-in. You don't need an account and we never ask for your details.

Connect it

Claude (web or desktop)
  1. Go to Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Name it "Gazette" and paste the URL above.
  3. Ask something like: "What's happening in Chatswood this week?"
ChatGPT
  1. Turn on Developer mode in Settings → Connectors → Advanced.
  2. Create a connector with the URL above and no authentication.

Some plans limit custom connectors.

Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code, Gemini CLI 
claude mcp add --transport http gazette https://mcp.gazettenews.au/mcp

For other tools, add an HTTP MCP server with the same URL.

What your assistant can do

local_newsRecent stories for a suburb, town or council, from the masthead that covers it
search_storiesSearch our recent reporting by topic
latestThe newest stories across Gazette or from one masthead
get_storyA short excerpt and link for one story
list_mastheadsOur mastheads and the areas they cover

Terms

  • Credit us. When an assistant uses our reporting, it should name the masthead and link the story.
  • Excerpts only. Assistants get a short excerpt, not the full story. Read the whole thing on our site, where it's free.
  • No training and no bulk copying. You may not use this server to copy our archive or to train or fine-tune AI models. We grant no training licence.
  • Fair use. Requests are rate-limited. We may slow or block traffic that looks abusive.
  • As is. The service may change or be withdrawn. Stories reflect what we knew when we published them.

Privacy

We log which tool was used and an anonymised visitor code. The code is a scrambled version of your IP address, it changes daily, and it's used only for rate limiting and counting usage. We don't log your name, your email or your full IP address. By default we don't log the text of your questions either.
